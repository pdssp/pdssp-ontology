Introduction back to ToC

Planetary science data products (images, digital elevation models, spectra, etc.) are distributed today under a wide range of mission- and archive-specific vocabularies: PDS3 keywords, PDS4 labels, EPN-TAP2's dataproduct_type/processing_level, CODMAC's own data-level scale, and more. Two catalogues describing the same real product commonly disagree on what to call it, purely because each grew its own local vocabulary independently.

The PDSSP Thesaurus gives the Planetary Data and Services System Platform (PDSSP) one shared, dereferenceable vocabulary for three orthogonal facets of a data product:

  • Product type (e.g. Image, Orthoimage, Digital elevation model, Point cloud, Anaglyph) -- what kind of object the product is;
  • Method (e.g. Panchromatic imaging, Multispectral imaging, Photogrammetry, Lidar, Radar imaging) -- how it was acquired or derived;
  • Processing level (Raw, Calibrated, Derived, Ancillary) -- how far it has been processed from raw telemetry, aligned with PDS4's own four-value scale.

Each concept carries skos:exactMatch/skos:closeMatch links to the USGS Thesaurus where an equivalent concept already exists there, and each Processing Level concept additionally records the corresponding EPN-TAP2, CODMAC, ESA PSA, NASA PDS, PDS3 and PDS4 code, so a consumer speaking any one of those systems can cross-walk to PDSSP's own value.

The thesaurus is consumed directly by PDSSP's own STAC catalogues: the stacplanet STAC extension's product:type and pdssp:method fields, attached to every STAC Item and Collection PDSSP publishes, take their values from this vocabulary's skos:prefLabels.
